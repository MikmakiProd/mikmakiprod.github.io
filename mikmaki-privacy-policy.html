Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy covers the Mikmaki Interactive website, mikmakiprod.github.io. Each of our games and apps has its own privacy policy, linked at the bottom of this page.

1. No account, no tracking

The website has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no cookies. We do not collect your name, email address or any other personal information when you visit it.

2. Language choice

The language you pick is saved in your browser (local storage) so the site opens in that language next time. It stays on your device and is never sent to us.

3. Hosting and fonts

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. Like any web server, GitHub may record technical data such as your IP address for security and to keep the service running. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Google Fonts delivers the fonts used on the site and receives your IP address to do so. See the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Links to other sites

The site links to the App Store and TikTok. Once you follow those links, their own privacy policies apply.

5. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

6. Contact

Mikmaki Interactive, mikmakimaki@gmail.com
