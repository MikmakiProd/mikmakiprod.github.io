About

Mikmaki Interactive is a tiny independent studio between Paris and Saigon.

Behind it is one person making small playful experiences and apps. Some are here to challenge you, like Rover and its walls that never stop coming. Others bring a little tenderness, like DOO, the small companion who counts on you every day. And some are simply useful, like Ôn tập, for reviewing Vietnamese a few minutes a day.
